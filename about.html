ABOUT M/S JAMEEL SONS

A Dependable Healthcare Partner

Bridging international clinical quality standards with responsive domestic supply chain coordination across Pakistan since 2018.

OUR BACKGROUND

Serving Pakistan’s Healthcare Infrastructure

Headquartered in the historic commercial hub of Multan, M/S Jameel Sons was founded to eliminate the procurement bottlenecks faced by medical institutions, clinical laboratories, and operating theaters.

Over the past 8+ years, we have built a reputation for zero-compromise product validation, rapid emergency dispatches, and ethical business conduct. Today, we manage an annual turnover of PKR 300 Million, supplying more than 50 government and private hospitals across Pakistan.

Our comprehensive range covers pharmaceuticals, high-precision laboratory diagnostics, sterile surgical disposables, personal protective wear, cardiac care implants and consumables, and general hospital supplies.
